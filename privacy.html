Privacy

Short, because there isn't much to say.

This website

vibebunch.com has no accounts, no forms and no tracking cookies. It is a plain page hosted on GitHub Pages.

The members page

When you sign in to the members page, with Google, GitHub or an emailed code, we receive your name and email address. We use them for one thing: checking that you're on the members list so we can let you in.

We don't sell them, share them or send you anything because of them. Sign-in is handled by Cloudflare, which keeps a log of sign-ins as part of running the service.

Questions or removal

Ask in the group chat or tell the host at the next brunch, and we'll take you off the members list.
