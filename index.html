Vibe Bunch

A vibe coding group in Longmont and Boulder, Colorado.

What this is

We get together about once a month, take turns hosting, and build things with AI assistance. Some of us have been writing software for years. Some of us started last month. Both are welcome, and the mix is the point.

Sessions are informal. People bring whatever they are working on, we build, and we show each other what worked and what did not.

Joining

We are a small group and we are happy to grow. If you are in the Longmont or Boulder area and this sounds like your kind of evening, get in touch and come to the next one.

You do not need experience. You do not need to bring a project. Turning up is enough.
