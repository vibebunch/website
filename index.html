Beginners welcome Longmont & Boulder

Saturday mornings are for making things.

A monthly brunch where locals build with AI. Some of us have written software for years, some started last month, and everyone brings something to show.

How a morning goes

Coffee and hellos

Introductions, and a quick round of what everyone is working on.

Laptops open

Build something. Pair up, ask for help, or keep going on your own project.

Show and tell

Show each other what worked, and what didn't. Half-broken demos count.

A different member hosts each month, alternating Longmont and Boulder.

The mix is the point.

Started last month?

You don't need to know how to code. Plenty of us learned by asking an AI and trying again.

Writing software for years?

Come for the new tools. Explaining what you know is the fastest way to learn it again.
